RELEASE MODE

INPUTS (isolated review)

This is a fresh, isolated review chat. You have no prior conversation history, no shared
reasoning, and no working sandbox from the document-generation run. The final package files
(generator.py, manifest.json, generator_report.json) and the source scan arrive as chat
attachments; the orchestrator-built visual-review envelope (independent review coverage, 17 per-edge
decisions, and hashes) is provided as pasted text or in an attached audit_evidence.md file. In that
envelope, package_artifacts hashes were recomputed independently by the orchestrator, while
reviewed_artifacts hashes are model-claimed — treat the latter as claims. Save the attached
generator.py and independently compile, import, and run it yourself. Do not trust report or handoff
claims that you cannot confirm against the attached artifacts. Any assigned issue IDs listed as
unresolved must be verified one by one against the attachments before you may report them verified;
if evidence is missing, fail rather than assume.
